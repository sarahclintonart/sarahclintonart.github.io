Sarah Clinton is a Nashville-based painter whose work explores the emotional presence of a moment, from figures in motion to the changing atmosphere of a landscape. Working in painting and mixed media, she is drawn to the subtle gestures, familiar scenes, and fleeting experiences that connect us to one another.
